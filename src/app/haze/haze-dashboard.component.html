About this data

Readings are model estimates from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS), served by Open-Meteo on a grid of roughly 45 km. They capture regional smoke well but can differ from ground stations, especially close to fires or in dense city centres. The map refreshes every 30 minutes while the page is open.

Values use the US EPA Air Quality Index. National indices are calculated differently, so Singapore's PSI, Malaysia's API and Indonesia's ISPU won't match these numbers exactly.

Official readings and advisories

  • Singapore: NEA haze updates
  • Malaysia: Air Pollutant Index (APIMS)
  • Indonesia: Air Pollution Standard Index (ISPU)
  • Thailand: Air4Thai
  • ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre: regional haze and hotspots
  • NASA FIRMS: active fire map
